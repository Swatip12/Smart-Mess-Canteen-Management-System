About Smart Mess & Canteen Management System

Smart Mess & Canteen Management System is a web-based application developed to simplify and manage mess and canteen related activities in an efficient and organized way.

Our System

The system provides a centralized platform for managing students, messes, canteens, menus, food items, orders, payments, subscriptions, attendance, feedback, complaints and notices.

Purpose

The main purpose of this system is to reduce manual work and provide a simple digital solution for managing daily mess and canteen operations.

Key Features

  • Student Management
  • Mess and Canteen Management
  • Menu and Food Item Management
  • Food Order Management
  • Payment and Subscription Management
  • Attendance Management
  • Feedback and Complaint Management
  • Notice Management
  • Dashboard and Reports
